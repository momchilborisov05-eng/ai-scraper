How to read this

Dev pages (10) were used while the extractors were built and tuned, so their scores are optimistic. Held-out pages (4: blog, forum, spec sheet, wiki table) were written after tuning and run once; one general fix (records with an extra modifier class, like a pinned forum row) was made after seeing them, and that is the only change.

Heuristic gets only the column names and types. Induction-1 and induction-2 also get 1 or 2 example rows typed by the user and learn exact rules from them.

Claude and hybrid modes cost money, so this page does not re-run them. They were measured once with scripts/benchmark.py --claude; the numbers and the cost are in the README.
